# Privacy information — Hope & Care Bangladesh

This website may collect contact messages and volunteer applications submitted by visitors. When configured, submissions are stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the organization. Administrator uploads are stored in Google Drive.

Please do not submit sensitive personal, financial, identity, or beneficiary information through public forms. Upload only materials that are appropriate for public viewing and for which the organization has permission to publish. Uploaded media is configured for anyone-with-the-link viewing so that public website visitors can access it.

The organization should set its own retention, access-review, consent, safeguarding, and deletion procedures before launch. This page is a starter notice and is not legal advice.
