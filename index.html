01 WHO WE ARE

Care is more than a feeling.
It’s something we do.

Hope & Care Bangladesh is a community-based social initiative committed to supporting vulnerable people and creating meaningful opportunities through grassroots action, volunteer engagement, and meaningful partnerships.
