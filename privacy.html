Privacy Policy

This page explains how Hope & Care Bangladesh intends to handle information associated with this website.

Important notice

This is an initial policy draft. The website's contact and volunteer forms are currently disabled and are not configured to submit information. Before enabling any form or backend, this policy must be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual information collected, storage arrangements, access permissions, and retention practices.

1. About this website

Hope & Care Bangladesh is an organization based in Shalbahan, Tetulia, Panchagarh, Bangladesh. This website is intended to provide information about the organization, its activities, announcements, and ways to get in touch.

2. Information you may provide

If website forms are enabled in the future, they may request information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, areas of interest, availability, or the message you choose to send. Only information necessary for the stated purpose should be requested.

Please do not send passwords, bank details, payment card information, or other sensitive personal information through general contact forms.

3. How information may be used

Information voluntarily submitted to the organization may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate about volunteering, or follow up on a request. Any actual use must be explained clearly when information is collected.

4. Storage and access

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and service providers may process technical information as part of delivering the website. No claim is made here that future form submissions are already being stored securely or that a particular database is operational.

Before a form backend is introduced, the organization should document where submissions are stored, who can access them, how access is protected, and how long the information is retained.

5. Sharing of information

The organization should not disclose personal information except where necessary for the purpose explained to you, with appropriate authorization, or where disclosure is required by applicable law. The actual arrangements must be confirmed before this policy is published as final.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The language switcher on this website may use your browser's local storage to remember your language preference. This is a browser-side preference, not a contact form submission. Hosting or third-party services may have their own technical practices.

7. Your choices and requests

If you have sent information to the organization through another channel and want to ask about it, contact us using the details below. We will need to confirm our actual procedures for access, correction, or deletion requests before promising a particular outcome.

8. Children's privacy

If an activity involves children, information and photographs should be collected or published only under an appropriate safeguarding process and with any consent required by the circumstances and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This draft should be updated whenever the website's forms, data collection, storage systems, or organizational practices change. A publication or revision date should be added when the policy is reviewed and approved.
